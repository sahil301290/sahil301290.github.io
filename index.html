Dr. Sahil Sharma

PhD, AFHEA | Postdoctoral Researcher in Applied AI

Hello and welcome to my personal page! I'm Dr. Sahil Sharma, a Postdoctoral Research Associate at the Intelligent Systems Research Centre (ISRC), Ulster University, UK. My research focuses on Explainable AI (XAI), Healthcare AI, and Computer Vision.

I am currently working on the AI-EPOCMON project, a US–Ireland R&D Partnership funded by NSF and UKRI. My work involves developing explainable machine learning models for cardiac troponin I diagnostics using Raman/SERRS spectroscopy.

Explore my research, publications, and academic journey to learn more about my contributions to the field of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning.
