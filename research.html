Research

Research Interests, Projects & Collaborations

My research lies at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence, Healthcare Informatics, and Computer Vision. I am particularly interested in developing explainable and trustworthy AI systems that can be deployed in real-world clinical settings.

Research Areas

Explainable AI (XAI)

Developing interpretable machine learning models using SHAP, LIME, and novel spectral explanation methods (SpecReX). Focus on making AI decisions transparent for clinical use.

Healthcare AI

AI-driven diagnostics for cardiac troponin I detection, myocardial infarction diagnosis, and epilepsy drug response prediction using spectroscopy and multimodal medical data.

Computer Vision

3D face reconstruction, occlusion-invariant face recognition, pose estimation, and deepfake detection using deep learning approaches.

Generative Models

GANs, VAEs, and Conditional GMMs for synthetic data generation, particularly for HDLSS (High Dimension, Low Sample Size) spectroscopy data augmentation.

Current Project

AI-EPOCMON: AI-enabled Point-of-Care Monitoring

Role: Lead AI and XAI Work-Packages

Funding: US–Ireland R&D Partnership (NSF, UKRI, SFI)

Period: Aug 2023 – Present

This project focuses on developing AI-enabled point-of-care diagnostic tools for myocardial infarction detection. My responsibilities include:

  • Developing explainable ML models for cardiac troponin I (cTnI) classification from SERRS spectra
  • Building conditional Gaussian Mixture Models for synthetic Raman spectra generation
  • Creating XAI visualization frameworks for multimodal medical data
  • Collaborating with Texas A&M University and Dublin City University

Past Research Projects

Ph.D. Research: 3D Face Recognition (2016–2021)

My doctoral research developed metaheuristic approaches for occlusion-invariant 3D face recognition. Key contributions included:

  • Voxel-based 3D face reconstruction using sequential deep learning
  • Game theory and simulated annealing for occluded face recognition
  • Variational autoencoders with triplet loss for face restoration
  • Transfer learning for gender and occlusion classification in 2.5D face images
  • Created the Colour-Rendered Bosphorus Projections (CRBP) benchmark dataset

IIT Mandi Project: Snow Avalanche Forecasting (2015)

Worked as Project Associate on a DRDO-SASE funded project for snow avalanche detection and forecasting using machine learning techniques.
