About Me

Education, Experience & Background

I am a Postdoctoral Research Associate at the Intelligent Systems Research Centre (ISRC), Ulster University, UK. I completed my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Thapar Institute of Engineering and Technology, India, where my research focused on metaheuristic approaches for occlusion-invariant 3D face recognition.

With over seven years of academic experience, I have held teaching positions at multiple institutions in India including Punjab Engineering College, JUIT, and TIET. I am passionate about applying AI/ML to healthcare problems and developing explainable systems that can be trusted in clinical settings.
